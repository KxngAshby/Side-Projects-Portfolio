Invite-only salary-cap fantasy

Owner's Box

Run the franchise. Live with the payroll.

Contract offers, cap space, and real NFL weeks — not a snake draft at 8pm.

The idea

Every owner runs a franchise under a $1000 salary cap. You don’t draft in a snake. You shop the open market with contract offers, manage payroll, set lineups, and live with short deals that force real roster decisions. Weekly scores pay $0.75 per fantasy point into a spendable performance bank.

How you join

  1. Open the app and sign in with email.
  2. Create a league (you’re commissioner) or join with an invite.
  3. Your seat stays on your account — same live room on any phone.

How you build a team

  • Invite only — share the league invite; friends claim a seat after signing in. No fixed team cap; the league grows with who joins.
  • Free agency — opens last week of NFL preseason, freezes 3 days before kickoff, then reopens in-season. After that, shopping stays open.
  • Public offers — rivals see your bid and can raise. Highest offer wins when the board settles.
  • Pricing — asks / OVR lean on last season’s Half-PPR PPG (rookies stay cheap). Tap a name for headshot + season stats.
  • Defense is a unit — buy one whole D/ST for your franchise.
  • Contracts in games — 90+ OVR → 1G, 80s → 2G, 70s → 3G, under 70 → 4G.

How weeks work

Scoring is ESPN Half-PPR. Lineup: QB, 2RB, 2WR, TE, FLEX, K, D/ST. No “simulate week” — the app syncs the real NFL scoreboard.

  • Lineup hard lock — Saturday 11:59 PM America/Chicago. No starter edits until the week finals / unlock.
  • FA stays open after lock — new buys are for next week once lineups unlock.
  • Playoffs — top 4 after Week 14 → semis Week 15 → championship Week 16.

What you’ll do each week

  1. Check cap space, injuries, and other rosters.
  2. Shop leftover-cap free agents (or sit tight).
  3. Set your lineup before Saturday 11:59 PM CT.
  4. Watch live scores track the NFL.
  5. Trade if you dare — both owners + commissioner.
