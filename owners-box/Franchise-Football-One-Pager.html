Invite-only salary-cap fantasy

Owner's Box

Run the franchise. Live with the payroll.

Contract offers, cap space, and real NFL weeks — not a snake draft at 8pm.

The idea

Every owner runs a franchise under a $800 salary cap. You don’t draft in a snake. You shop the open market with contract offers, manage payroll, set lineups, and live with short deals that force real roster decisions.

How you build a team

  • Invite only — put in the code, name your club, you’re in. No fixed 20-team cap; the league grows with who joins.
  • Free agency windows — main shopping opens the last week of NFL preseason, freezes 3 days before kickoff, then reopens in-season if you still have cap left.
  • Public offers — when someone puts money on a player, the league sees it. You can raise. Highest offer wins when the board settles.
  • Defense is a unit — buy one whole D/ST for your franchise.
  • Contracts in games — 90+ OVR → 2G, 80s → 3G, 70s → 4G, under 70 → 5G. Form can bump live OVR and next-deal cost; signed deals stay until they expire.

How weeks work

Scoring is ESPN Half-PPR. You set a classic lineup (QB, 2RB, 2WR, TE, FLEX, K, D/ST). There is no “simulate week” button — the app syncs the real NFL scoreboard. When the NFL slate is final, your fantasy week closes.

What you’ll actually do each week

  1. Check cap space and injuries.
  2. Shop leftover-cap free agents (or sit tight).
  3. Set your lineup before kickoff.
  4. Watch live scores track the NFL.
  5. Trade if you dare — both owners agree, commissioner approves.
